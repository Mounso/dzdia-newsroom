# Roadmap

Where the project stands and what comes next. The plan follows the product spec v2.1 (private document); every new module starts with an ADR.

## Principles

- **Measure before building.** Each evaluation set is built before the feature it measures.
- **Workflows where the steps are known, an agent only where the path depends on what it finds.** The morning briefing is a scheduled workflow; `/veille <topic>` is the only true agent loop.
- **Human approval before any write.** Nothing reaches WordPress without a persisted approval.

**Order changed on 2026-11-15.** The morning briefing (week 4) moves up: it comes right after the stage 2 slice, ahead of the rest of the week 2 article flow (synthesis card, angle choice, approval workflow, WordPress draft) and of week 3. The reason: the team is said to find most of its topics in social media feeds, and 160 of the outlet's 573 posts embed an Instagram post ([corpus counters](evals/dzdia-corpus.md#results)), so help with finding topics may save more time than help with writing. This is not confirmed by the journalists yet; the discovery questions below ask them. The `/veille` agent and the guardrails keep their place.

**Direction after the journalists' answers (2026-11-15).** They describe an article as the end of a chain: a social media post is spotted, the topic is documented, the person is interviewed, then the article is written. They want a long article with quotes and its sources, and a briefing that gives a summary and an angle with each link. The outlet's own articles agree on length and quotes ([corpus counters](evals/dzdia-corpus.md#results)). One web page is therefore not the material of an article: the stage 2 slice serves the documentation step. It is finished in its lightest form ([ADR-0019](adr/0019-lightest-wiring-of-the-slice.md)); the effort then moves to the morning briefing, and to the interview as an input of the article flow. Both need answers that are still missing: what can be read from Instagram, and the language and the form of the interview recordings.

A guard on quotes follows from that result ([ADR-0014](adr/0014-quote-must-be-quoted-in-source.md)): a quote is kept only when the source puts its words inside quotation marks. It is written, and replayed on the recorded drafts it rejects 5 of the 16 kept quotes, the 2 misattributed ones included ([replay](evals/v2-comparison.md#guard-replay-2026-11-14)); its prompt, `v2-article-2`, was tried on the six pages outside the sample and frozen on 2026-11-14 ([tryout](evals/v2-tryout.md#v2-article-2-2026-11-14)). A local copy of the outlet's own articles was collected on 2026-11-14 as a style reference ([ADR-0015](adr/0015-dzdia-corpus-local-copy.md), [counters](evals/dzdia-corpus.md)). The place of the architecture was decided on 2026-11-15 ([ADR-0017](adr/0017-v2-stays-the-production-path.md)): the v2 pipeline stays the production path of the slice, for code-checked quotes and traceable sources, not for a lower rate, and the choice is revisited once the journalists have answered. It is tested without the network; no real page has been fetched yet. It is tested without the network and without a real model. Next: a first real page fetch and an end-to-end run with the development bot. Target: 2026-11-22 at the latest. |
